Weekly

Main Service

Saturdays 10:00 AM

Our Saturday gathering is the heartbeat of Dharmikta Mandali — a time for worship, the Word, prayer, and community fellowship.

Our main service is the cornerstone of our weekly gatherings, designed to strengthen your faith and connect you with our church family.

What to expect

Heartfelt worship, powerful biblical teaching, and altar prayer. We also hold dedicated fellowship for children, women, youth, and fathers — ensuring everyone finds encouragement and belonging.

Weekly

Women's Ministry

Wednesdays 10:30 AM

A supportive community for women to grow spiritually and build meaningful relationships rooted in faith.

Our Women's Ministry provides a safe space for women of all ages to connect, share, and deepen their faith journey together.

In detail

Weekly Bible study, intimate prayer, and encouraging fellowship. Annual combined events and mentorship programs nurture spiritual growth and lasting friendships.

Monthly

Father's Fellowship

Third Saturday After service

Equipping fathers to lead with integrity in their families, workplaces, and communities.

Focused on building godly character and practical tools for biblical fatherhood.

Program highlights

Monthly gathering for sharing, encouragement, and prayer — creating an environment for spiritual sharpening, mutual accountability, and faith-based leadership.

Weekly

Children's Ministry

Saturdays 10:00 AM

A fun, safe environment where children discover God's love through age-appropriate activities and teaching.

Engaging programs that help children build a strong spiritual foundation while having fun.

Our programs

Interactive Bible lessons, Christian films, and learning quizzes. Special programs and celebrations help children grow in faith and friendships from an early age.

Monthly

Youth Ministry

Saturdays After service

A vibrant community where teens and young adults explore faith, build friendships, and grow spiritually.

A relevant space for teenagers to connect with God and with each other.

What we do

Weekly prayer, monthly programs and fellowship, and annual mission trips. Leadership training helps young people grow into confident, faith-filled adults.

Annual

Pillars Academy

6-month program

An intensive discipleship program for those seeking deeper biblical knowledge and leadership development.

A transformative program equipping participants for effective ministry and personal spiritual growth.

Program components

Biblical teaching five days a week, daily devotions, personal mentorship, church service opportunities, and occasional retreats for holistic growth.

Annual

Vocational Training

6-month program

Practical skills training integrated with biblical principles for workplace excellence and honest living.

Preparing participants for meaningful employment while emphasising Christian work ethics.

Training areas

Hands-on instruction in tailoring and fashion design, animal husbandry, and agriculture — each rooted in biblical principles of integrity and excellence.
