Weekly

Main Service

Saturdays 10:00 AM

The main weekly gathering of Dharmikta Mandali, held every Saturday morning at 10:00 AM in Bhaisepati, Lalitpur.

The service begins with hymn singing, followed by a time for testimonies from members of the congregation. Worship then continues before the preacher brings the sermon from the Word of God. The service closes with a time of prayer.

Sub-fellowships

During the main service, children attend their own separate fellowship held at the same time. After the main service concludes, the women's, youth's, and fathers' fellowships is held separately on their respective scheduled days.

Weekly

Women's Fellowship

Wednesdays 11:00 AM

A weekly gathering for the women of the church is held every Wednesday at 11:00 AM.

The women of the church meet every Wednesday morning. It is a time set aside for reading from the Bible, praying together, and sitting with one another. Members share from what they are going through, and the group prays over those things together. Monthly Woman Fellowship is also held after the main service on the third Saturday of each month.

Combined gatherings

From time to time, the women's fellowship also holds larger combined gatherings that bring together women from the church's branch congregations. These events usually involve extended worship, teaching, and a shared meal.

Monthly

Father's Fellowship

Third Saturday After service

A monthly gathering for the Father of the church is held after the main service on the third Saturday of each month.

The Father's Fellowship meets once a month on the third Saturday, right after the main service ends. The gathering is a time for the Father of the congregation to sit together, read from the Bible, discuss the Word, and pray. Topics often center on faith, family, and how to walk uprightly in daily life.

Format

The session usually includes a short teaching or reading, open sharing among the Fathers present, and a closing time of prayer. It is an informal setting where members speak honestly about the challenges and responsibilities they carry.

Weekly

Children Fellowship

Saturdays 10:00 AM

A separate Saturday gathering for children runs at the same time along side the main service.

While the main service is going on, the children meet separately in another room. The session follows its own order and is kept suitable for the age of the children attending.

What takes place

The children's session includes Bible stories told in simple language, Christian films, and small quiz activities based on what they have heard. Special programs are held on occasions such as Christmas and Easter, where children take part in performances and recitations.

Monthly

Youth Fellowship

Saturdays After service

A monthly gathering for the young people of the church is held after the main Saturday service.

The youth meet once a month after the main Saturday service. The gathering is a time to read scripture, talk openly about questions related to faith, and pray. Outside of the monthly meeting, the youth also gather separately for prayer during the week.

Other activities

Beyond the regular monthly meeting, the youth occasionally organise outings, community service activities, and annual programs. Some members have also participated in short-term mission visits to other areas.

Annual

Pillars Academy

February – July / August

Annually (Generally from February through to July or August), Dharmikta Mandali runs a 6-month biblical discipleship training.

Pillars Academy is a discipleship Training that runs for approximately six months each year, starting around February. It is open to those who wish to study the Bible more deeply and grow in their understanding of Christian faith and ministry.

Program structure

Teaching is held five days a week. Alongside classroom sessions, students take part in daily devotions, personal mentoring with a church leader, and practical service within the church. Occasional retreats are also held during the program period.

Annual

Vocational Training

February – July / August

A six-month skills training program is held alongside Pillars Academy, offering practical instruction in tailoring, animal husbandry, and agriculture.

The Vocational Training program runs during the same period as Pillars Academy (from around February to July or August each year). It provides hands-on skills training in areas such as tailoring and fashion design, animal husbandry, and agriculture. The program is grounded in the church's belief that honest work and good character go hand in hand.

Training areas

Participants receive practical instruction in one or more of the following: tailoring and basic fashion design, livestock care and animal husbandry, and small-scale farming and agriculture. Biblical teaching on integrity and stewardship is woven into the program alongside the skills training.
