Dharmikta Mandali

Worship, Discipleship and Service

Dharmikta Mandali is a Christ-centered church that has been nurturing a community of believers through teaching, outreach, and love since 2009 AD. We believe in the unity of believers and the power of prayer to transform lives and communities.

Our Mission

Mission

To expand the Kingdom of Christ: proclaiming the Gospel to the lost, making disciples, equipping servants (leaders), sending missionaries to unreached places, and planting churches. (Matthew 28:19-20)

Our Vision

Vision

"Let your manner of life be worthy of the gospel of Christ - that you are standing firm in one spirit, with one mind striving side by side for the faith of the gospel, fulfilling the Great Commission of Christ." (Philippians 1:27)

Our Goals

Goals

  • Glorify God

    To give all honour, respect, dignity and glory to the Lord Jesus Christ — through the mediating name of God the Father, in the power of the Holy Spirit.

  • Grow God's Kingdom

    To be dedicated to the growth of God's Kingdom, founded on the Holy Scriptures of the Bible. (Philippians 4:11)

  • Build Up the Church

    To support, uplift, and guide the church toward spiritual maturity according to the Holy Scriptures, and to engage continuously in the salvation of souls.

  • Witness to the World

    To demonstrate the Christian (spiritual) flavour and light of Christ to the world through word and conduct, as commanded in the Holy Scriptures.

What We Believe

Statement of Faith

  • The Holy Scripture

    The Old and New Testaments constitute the sufficient revelation of God; both sections of the Holy Scripture are God's revelation, written under the inspiration of the Holy Spirit. The Scripture is authoritative, illuminating, pure and complete in his content, with no errors in his teaching. The church accepts that Scripture is sufficient for Christian faith, Christian practical life, doctrine, and the teaching needed for salvation.

  • The Trinity

    Father, Son, and Holy Spirit are one triune God. The church accepts that the triune God is involved in the great works of creation, salvation, redemption, and forgiveness of sin.

  • Jesus Christ the Saviour

    The Lord Jesus Christ, Saviour, was born of the virgin Mary — fully God and fully Man. He shed his precious blood once for all as the atoning sacrifice on the cross of Calvary for the sins of all, died sacrificially in our place, rose bodily, and ascended to heaven. The church believes in his ongoing intercession for all the saints and in his physical, visible return.

  • The Holy Spirit

    The personhood of the Holy Spirit, his divinity, power, work, and renewal are necessary and sufficient in every believer: new birth, sanctification, fulfilment, resurrection, and glorification in bodily form. The church accepts that believers will experience the baptism and fullness of the Holy Spirit.

  • Humanity and Sin

    God created humanity in his own image and likeness and desired his glory, but humanity became disobedient, sinned, and fell completely. The church accepts that it is not possible for humanity to obtain redemption/salvation through its own efforts by doing good works.

  • Salvation by Grace

    The church believes that through the mediating work of God's Son, his grace and mercy, sinners receive complete freedom and salvation only through faith in him.

  • Justification and Baptism

    Sinners are justified only by God's grace through faith. The necessary steps to receive new birth and salvation are acknowledging oneself as a sinner, repentance, and faith. Those who are thus saved must be baptised in water in the name of God the Father, God the Son, and God the Holy Spirit, according to the command of Christ.

  • The Second Coming

    The church believes that Jesus Christ will one day raise the believers, reign, judge the dead and the living, grant believers entrance into the eternal kingdom, and bring eternal condemnation upon those who do not believe.

Our Story

Our History

Dharmikta Mandali was established in 2009 with 25 believers who gathered in a small rented room in Bhanjyang, Lalitpur. Through continuous prayer, God revealed Proverbs 14:34, which became the foundation for our name — Dharmikta Mandali (Righteousness Church).

Within a month the room was full, so we rented an additional space. Few months later, we had to expand again as more people joined the fellowship.

Since the church continued to grow, we moved to Bhaisepati, where we:

  • Firstly moved our church to a new space.
  • Later relocated to another rented land and built a new church building
  • Finally moved to our current church building built on our own land

Today we gather freely to worship and serve, continuing the journey that began with prayer and faith.
